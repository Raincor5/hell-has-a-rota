## OUR CORE VALUES

**INTEGRITY**

Honesty is the foundation of our organisation.

We expect all Operatives to tell the truth at all times.

Management reserves the right to provide an alternative version of events where operationally necessary.

**EQUALITY**

Everyone is equal here.

Some employees simply demonstrate exceptional potential through their close working relationships with senior personnel.

We ask that you respect their achievements.

**TRANSPARENCY**

Our open-door policy ensures every employee has a voice.

The door is locked.

The key is with Management.

Management is unavailable.

**TEAMWORK**

At the Eternal Service, we don't believe in individuals.

**WE ARE A TEAM!**

A supportive, close-knit team built on trust, mutual respect, and a shared commitment to discussing matters privately with absolutely everyone except the person concerned.

We celebrate each other's successes.

Particularly when they are undeserved.

We respect each other's boundaries.

Particularly when they are inconvenient.

And we never allow personal matters to interfere with professional decisions.

The recent promotions were entirely coincidental.

**PROFESSIONALISM**

We maintain the highest standards of workplace conduct.

Personal feelings must remain outside the Establishment.

Unless they are useful for operational purposes.

Please remember that what happens outside working hours is strictly private.

Unless Management has already heard about it.
